## What I learned

- **`gs.py` could not read a Google Doc at all** — and a *tabbed* doc is doubly invisible: a plain
  `documents().get()` returns nothing useful without `includeTabsContent=True`, and child tabs nest.
  Both the SM Captions doc and the CTB Story Drafts doc are tabbed. Fixed, not worked around:
  **`tools/gsuite/doc_tabs.py`** lists tabs and dumps any tab's paragraphs *with their character
  indices*, which is exactly what a `batchUpdate` needs to rewrite a block in place.
- **Edit by search, never by remembered index.** A live doc moves under you between sessions. The
  working pattern: find the block by matching its text → `deleteContentRange` → `insertText` at the
  same start → keep the last paragraph's trailing newline (`endIndex - 1`) so paragraph structure
  survives → and put `tabId` inside the range/location dict or the edit lands on the wrong tab.
- **The portraits pipeline has a real source now.** The CTB Story Drafts doc carries one tab per
  education leader (17 of them, plus a "Next Edition" set). That's a standing supply of Thursday
  portrait captions, not a one-off — recorded on the captions page with the format that's holding.
- **The source tabs carry design instructions worth surfacing.** Champa's tab opens with *"blur the
  faces of the children wherever they are visible."* Caption work should keep reading past the prose
  and flag these to whoever builds the creative.
