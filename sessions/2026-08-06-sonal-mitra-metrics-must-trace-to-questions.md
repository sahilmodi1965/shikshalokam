# MItra survey — every metric must trace to a question

## What got made
Audited all 29 dashboard rows and the full report spec against the questionnaire, then rebuilt the
instrument so nothing is sourced from something the survey does not ask. All three revised tabs
regenerated in Sonal's template.

## What we learned
- **Sonal's rule, and it is now the governing rule of this instrument:** *the questions lead to the
  dashboard. If there is no question, the dashboard cannot create it.* She found it via **Story
  pipeline** and **Awards pipeline**, which sat on the dashboard with nothing in the conversation
  behind them.
- **Four metrics failed the audit,** not two: story pipeline, awards pipeline, action timeline and
  still live. All four were sourced from a "post-close screen" that existed only as a line in this
  repo, never as a MItra capability. It had quietly accumulated seven items.
- **Timing was the expensive one.** "When did it start, is it still going" is the chart carrying the
  survey's primary objective, so it could not live on an imaginary surface. It is a real turn again
  (Q6), funded by giving back the perception baseline added three days earlier.
- **The baseline was the right turn to surrender.** Both its ratings were collected inside the same
  conversation, so the "delta" was retrospective recall on a matched scale — only marginally
  stronger than asking how much someone shifted. Perception is a single self-rated magnitude
  question again, and the methodology note says so.
- **Story willingness, awards nomination, follow-up contact and photo left the instrument.** They
  are operational asks, not impact measures. They move to the thank-you email that follows the
  survey, and no tile is built from them.
- **Partner origin is the remaining soft spot.** Real (Q4 probe, Yes branch) but buried in a
  four-part open answer with no backstop now. Report against a stated base.

## What to improve next
- Final set: profile · editions · perception (+probe) · action (+probe) · ripple & influence ·
  timing · continuity · future readiness = 10 turns.
- If registration data can pre-fill Q1/Q2, the first freed turn should buy the perception baseline
  back, and the second should split Q6 so timing and still-live are asked cleanly.
