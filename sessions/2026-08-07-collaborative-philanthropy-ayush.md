  no new claims invented, everything cited.

## What the page holds
- **Thesis** — funding isolated projects buys outputs; funding leadership, coherence and long-term
  institutional capacity builds a system that keeps improving after the grant ends.
- **Fund Orchestration** as one of six SL 2.0 capabilities + the standing Funding Orchestration Team;
  **Fund** as one of the 10 verbs, level with Amplify/Convene/Mentor — never the senior one.
- **Funder-role map** — Strategic / Anchor / Momentum Partners, Co-Builders, Advisors — as better
  vocabulary than "donor".
- **"Capital beyond money"** — trust, networks, relationships, knowledge, governance, shared
  ownership, from the InvokED 5.0 "Beyond Fundraising — Orchestrating Capital" Brain Date.
- **The three Strategic Partners Roundtable questions**, verbatim and reusable.
- **Evidence** — Punjab (13th → 1st, funded by Deloitte + A.T.E. Chandra), Nagaland (Education Above
  All), plus the WEF *Future is Collective* validation.
- **Six writing rules**, including "drop movement stats for global philanthropy audiences".

## What we learned
- The differentiator worth naming: in both Punjab and Nagaland the funder sits **inside** the
  collective with a role, not outside it with a reporting requirement. That framing wasn't written
  down anywhere before — it fell out of reading the case studies side by side.
- Our public vocabulary is **fund orchestration / orchestrating capital**, not "collaborative
  philanthropy". The page title may need to change once that's settled.
