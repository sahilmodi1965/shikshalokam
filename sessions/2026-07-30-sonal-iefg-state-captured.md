# IEFG application state captured into the brain, read-only

Sonal revised the IEFG membership application herself and asked the brain to read and save it,
explicitly making no changes. Done — the working state is now recorded so a later session picks up
where she left off rather than re-deriving it.

## What got made
- **`wiki/sources/iefg-membership-application-2026.md`** — the application's current answers, the
  edits Sonal made and what they signal, everything still open, and the numbers in use.
- Filed as **`sensitivity: internal`**. The application is confidential to the IEFG Steering
  Committee, and it carries budget, staffing and an unresolved eligibility question. It went to
  `wiki/` deliberately, **not** to a project page, because project pages publish to the public site.

## What we learned
- **"Read and save, make no changes" is its own instruction and worth honouring exactly.** The value
  was in capturing a moving document at a moment when the author had just finished shaping it, not
  in improving it.
- **The right home for a record depends on where it publishes, not on what kind of thing it is.**
  A project page would have been the natural place for a live piece of work, and would have put
  confidential material on a public URL. `wiki/` is private; `sessions/` and `projects/` are not.
  Worth checking that distinction every time something sensitive gets filed.
- **Author edits carry signal beyond the words.** Several of Sonal's changes were substantive rather
  than stylistic, and are recorded as such so nobody reverses them later thinking they were slips.
