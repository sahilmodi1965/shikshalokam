## The eligibility problem, flagged and unresolved
IEFG is *"for foundations and philanthropic organisations who give grants to other organisations;
it is not open to grantseekers or NGOs seeking funding."* ShikshaLokam Foundation is a Section 8
non-profit whose operational costs are covered by SFPI — a **recipient** of philanthropy and an
implementer. On IEFG's own definition **SFPI is the eligible entity, not ShikshaLokam.**

The Oxford report makes this sharper, because it is published and independent: it describes SL as
*"an NGO founded under the aegis of Shibulal Family Philanthropic Initiatives"* and an *"education
leadership catalyst."* Not a grantmaker. Anyone on the Steering Committee who searches will find that
characterisation.

**Q2e — percentage of the education portfolio granted to external organisations — is still blank,**
and it is the number that decides the application. Also unresolved: whether Momentum Partners
receive funds from SL or bring their own. Options put to Sonal: apply as SFPI; ask IEFG where an
operating philanthropy fits; or don't tick "we grant-make."

## What we learned
- **A .docx in Drive cannot be safely co-edited.** The form was an uploaded Word file, so the Docs
  API couldn't touch it. Editing the XML and re-uploading worked, but when Sonal edited in Google
  Docs at the same time, her text and mine interleaved *at character level* — the question itself
  came back as "b) Do you eeducation leadership catalystng activities?". She recreated the form as a
  native Doc, which fixed it permanently. **Rule: if a form arrives as .docx, convert it before any
  collaborative filling.**
- **The brain had no external validation asset until today.** Everything about Shikshagraha was
  self-authored. An independent Oxford playbook, with named authors and a public PDF, is worth more
  to a funder than any of our own numbers, and nothing in the brain pointed to it.
- **Third-party descriptions can contradict our own claims.** Oxford calling SL an NGO and a
  catalyst is accurate and useful, and it also undercuts a grantmaking claim. Worth checking what
  published sources say about us *before* ticking eligibility boxes.
