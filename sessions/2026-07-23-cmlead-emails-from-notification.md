# What got made

Both CM LEAD selection-outcome emails moved from placeholder-heavy drafts to near-final, sourced
from the official Notification Doc (`1keCkTnbb...`) and its comments.

- **Email 1 split into two.** The Notification puts the shortlist in two batches on two dates
  (Annexure-I on 30 July, Annexure-II on 31 July), so the single broadcast became two mails with
  identical bodies apart from the date.
- **Filled from the notice:** physical interview, MBOSE Regional Office venue address, 9:00 AM
  report time with a day running to 7:00 or 8:00 PM, originals plus one set of self-attested
  photocopies, 12 Fellows as 8 Meghalaya + 4 other states, and "shortlisting is not selection".
- **Slot PDF dropped.** No longer needed once the mail carries venue and report time.
- **Sender changed to Team CM LEAD**, not a named officer. That retired the `{{SPD NAME}}` blocker
  that had been holding two pieces of candidate-facing content.
- **Confirmation simplified** to "reply to this email within two days", rescheduling handled in the
  same reply. No fixed date, no separate contact person.
- **Two Doc comments applied:** the duplicate Medashisha Khongwir entry (count is 44, not 45) and
  the reframe of "venue communicated separately" now that the address is in the mail.

# What we learned

- **A notification and its cover email are not the same artefact.** The notice can say "details will
  follow separately"; the email is the thing a candidate acts on, so it has to carry venue, time and
  documents outright. Syed's comment caught exactly this seam.
- **An operational fact can change a content structure.** A batch split in an annexure turned one
  broadcast into two mails. Worth checking source documents for structure, not just for values.
- **Institutional senders beat named ones when the name is unavailable.** Signing as Team CM LEAD
  unblocked content that had been waiting on an officer's name for two sessions.

# To improve next

- Read a source Doc's **comments** at the same time as its body. The comments held two corrections
  the body did not.
- When a count appears in both a list and a mail, check the list for duplicates before quoting it.
- Standing boundary reaffirmed: when a Google Doc is given as a **source**, read only. Changes land
  in the brain's own pages unless someone explicitly asks for the Doc to be edited.
