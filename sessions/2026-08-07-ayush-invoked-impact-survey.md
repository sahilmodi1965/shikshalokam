## What got made
- **[[invoked-impact-survey-plan-2026]]** — the build sheet for the InvokED Impact & Perception Survey
  absorbed as a source: 8 questions, 28 dashboard metrics, the per-conversation and aggregate report
  templates, and the five honesty rules that bind anything we write off the data.
- **`projects/invoked-impact-survey/page.md`** — new standalone campaign workspace. Two phases
  (get responses → the report and its runway), back-planned from a report due **30 Sep 2026**.

## What we decided
- **Standalone project page**, not folded into `invoked-6` or the paused Listening at Scale plan —
  different audience (alumni, not ecosystem) and a different clock.
- **Report by end of September** (Ayush). That fixes a **four-week field window, 17 Aug – 13 Sep**,
  and a prep week starting 10 Aug.

## What we learned
- The hard problem is the **response rate**, not the instrument: 150–250 completions from ~900 cold,
  senior, time-poor alumni is 20–25%. A single blast yields ~72 — a clear miss. Four waves with a
  genuinely personalised Wave 1 model out at ~185.
- **The build sheet has a give-back gap.** It is thorough on what the survey takes and silent on what the
  respondent gets. Closing it is a comms job. **Q8 (future readiness) is the strongest card** — it
  turns an impact survey into a peer study senior leaders would want to be counted in.
- The 6.0 **audience→signatory map is reusable** — the survey should arrive from the same person
  whose invite the alumnus accepted.
- Honesty rule 1 ("a No on Q4 is not an absence of impact") is worth saying **to the respondent**
  in the invite, not just in the report — it removes the reason a non-collaborator would skip.
