## What we learned — five corrections that reshaped it
1. **Scope** — runs to InvokED 6.0 as an introduction to the idea, not a 6-week MItra burst. Future
   readiness framed inside Viksit Bharat 2047.
2. **Spine** — not one child's story. One question, put to children, teachers, parents, community,
   and to leaders publicly.
3. **No numbers** — our chaupal/member/challenge figures aren't MItra voice-captures and aren't
   segregated by future readiness. The million is an internal February target only.
4. **Supply reality** — voices are few, arrive unevenly, and are **audio only, no video**. So: public
   report dropped, no voice cadence promised, audiogram/transcript/photograph replace film. And every
   post must carry a named perspective or a citable fact — nothing empty.
5. **Comms perspective only** — collection, consent and fieldwork belong to field/product. They're a
   named dependency in the plan, never a task in it.
