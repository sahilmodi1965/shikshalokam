## What we learned (the four corrections that reshaped it)
1. **Scope** — the campaign runs to InvokED 6.0 as an introduction to the idea, not a 6-week MItra
   burst. Future readiness sits inside the Viksit Bharat 2047 frame.
2. **Spine** — it's not one child's story. One question, asked of children, teachers, parents,
   community, and of leaders publicly.
3. **No numbers** — our chaupal/member/challenge figures are not MItra voice-captures and aren't
   segregated by future readiness. Campaign publishes no counts. The million is an internal
   February target only.
4. **Reality of supply** — voices are few, arrive unevenly, and are **audio only, no video**. So:
   public report dropped; no voice cadence promised; audiogram/transcript/photograph replace film.
   And every post must carry a named perspective or a citable fact — nothing empty.
