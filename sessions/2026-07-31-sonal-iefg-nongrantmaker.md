## What we learned
- **A position change has a blast radius.** The comments unticked two boxes; the consequence was
  prose in three unrelated sections that now contradicted the form's own checkboxes. **After any
  decision that changes what an organisation claims to be, grep the whole document for the old
  claim** rather than trusting the comment list to be complete.
- **Flag, then act on the answer.** Two of those three fixes were wording Sonal had never commented
  on, so they were raised in chat first rather than changed silently. Both were then approved in one
  line. Cheaper than either guessing or leaving a live contradiction in a submitted form.
- **Checkboxes in a Google Doc form live inside tables**, not in body paragraphs. A paragraph-level
  walker finds nothing; the working approach is to walk table rows and flip the ☒ in the cell to the
  left of the matching label, replacing a single character so indices never shift.
