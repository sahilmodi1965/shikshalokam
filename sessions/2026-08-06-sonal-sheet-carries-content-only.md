# Sheet carries content only — build rules stripped back out

## What got made
Stripped `Questions_Revised_10` back to the header, the 8 question rows and the TOTAL row.
Removed the build-rules block, and trimmed rationale and change-history language out of the
insight column on the questions tab and the comments column on the dashboard.

## What we learned
- **The 27 Jul rule is broader than "no change history": it is no rules either.** I had added a
  build-rules block to the questions tab in the same session that established the
  metrics-trace-to-questions rule. Sonal: *"dont write rules or other things on the sheet. Just the
  content that is needed."*
- The line: **build rules govern how the instrument is assembled, so they belong on the project
  page.** The tab carries questions, columns and metric rows. Nothing else. Operational text that
  the question itself needs to run — "asked of everyone whatever they answered in Q4", "anchored to
  Q4 or Q5" — stays, because that is the spec, not commentary.
- Same test applied to the dashboard comments column: kept the instruction ("state the base",
  "chart it", "cluster the barriers"), cut the justification.

## What to improve next
- Apply this before writing to any sheet, not after: the deliverable carries content, the terminal
  and the project page carry reasoning.
