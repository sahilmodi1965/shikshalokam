# Raw capture — 2026-07-17

## Bihar Dept of Education — MoU video transcript (shared by Ayush)
Official Bihar government video (Hindi) announcing MoUs the Department of Education signed,
at zero cost, with seven educational organisations. Transcript (Hindi + English) shared by Ayush
for the Bihar MoU LinkedIn post.

**English translation (from Ayush):**
- In Bihar, the land of knowledge, the future of education is going to become even stronger.
- The state government is taking another important step to ensure quality and modern education.
- The Department of Education is signing MoUs **at zero cost** with **seven** major educational
  institutions with world-class experience.
- **ekStep Foundation** — digital practice in Hindi, English, and Mathematics.
- **Wadhwani AI** — assess the reading ability of young children; identify students who lag behind.
- **Khan Academy India** — strengthen Mathematics and Science for classes 6 to 10.
- **Pi Jam Foundation** — introduce students to digital skills, Artificial Intelligence, and coding.
- **Mantra for Change** — experiential/experience-based education via project-based learning and
  mentoring programs across all schools.
- **Pratham Education Foundation** — improve foundational learning of language and mathematics.
- **Shiksha Lokam Foundation** — connect the feedback/opinions of **parents, teachers, and students**
  with the education system through an **AI-based framework**.
- These initiatives will ensure timely identification of children's learning gaps, promote regular
  practice, and ensure continuous improvement in the quality of education.

**SL people long associated with Bihar (from Ayush):** Khushboo Awasthi (Chief Curator, Shikshagraha),
Santosh More, Neeraj Doddamane (COO), Saurabh Singh.
