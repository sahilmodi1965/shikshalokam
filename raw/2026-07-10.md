=== TAB: Overview ===

Summary:
ShikshaLokam is an education leadership catalyst established in 2017 by the Shibulal Family
Philanthropic Initiatives. It works to enable and amplify leadership development across India's
public education system by restoring the agency of education leaders — their ability to drive
continuous micro-improvements in their context.
As a co-builder of Shikshagraha, a people-powered movement for education equity, ShikshaLokam
orchestrates collaboration across government, civil society, philanthropy, and communities to
strengthen leadership ecosystems across geographies.
Through digital public infrastructure like ELEVATE and AI capabilities such as MItra, ShikshaLokam
enables leaders to act, captures grassroots insights, and connects efforts across the system,
transforming local actions into collective intelligence and driving systemic improvement in public
education at scale, with speed, and sustainably.

Mission Statement:
Enabling and amplifying leadership development opportunities for individuals and institutions
engaged in K-12 education systems

The Anchor:
ShikshaLokam enables education leadership by restoring their agency — the ability of individuals to
drive continuous micro-improvements in their context.

Communication Goals:
1. Amplify the imperative role of education leadership in improving public schools (Restoring agency
   of leaders → continuous improvement → systemic transformation)
2. Strengthen ShikshaLokam's identity as the Shikshagraha movement builder — Catalyse and legitimise
   collective action by showcasing how leadership missions and partnerships are emerging and
   strengthening across states, districts and communities to solve education challenges together.
   Clearly articulate SL's role in: Government system orchestration; Network catalysis
3. Amplify ELEVATE — Digital public infrastructure (DPI) and AI capabilities (Mitra) as the tools to
   scale leadership development.

Pillar 1: Leadership with Agency in Action (Across Geographies)
Focus: Leadership is not abstract; it is practised daily through micro-improvements by individuals
across schools, districts, and states. Build a national narrative on education leadership by making
the agency visible through real actions (micro-improvements) across contexts.
What to communicate: how leaders (teachers, principals, officials, community members) exercise
agency; micro-improvements in real contexts; variations of leadership across geographies.
Content formats: ed-leader portraits (blogs & videos); coffee table book celebrating enabled
leadership; consumable formats of external studies & research (carousels, posters, infographics);
data-driven insights on empowered leadership (impact nos., survey rankings); blogs, impact stories,
pop-culture pieces & thought leadership.

Pillar 2: Shikshagraha as a Movement in Action
Focus: collective action across Samaaj, Sarkaar, Bazaar & Sanchaar; partnerships solving problems
across geographies; SL as system orchestrator + network catalyst.
Content formats: partnership/collective formation & meeting announcements; bright spots (partner &
government collaboration stories); data insights & movement progress (# active leaders, #
improvements triggered, # active states & districts, # partners); learning from a program/geography;
convening-to-action stories (how InvokED/Shiksha Samvad conversations translate into partnerships;
ideas → pilots → programs → mission [Mission SOAR]); Voices from the Movement (quote cards, 30-sec
video bytes — govt official, NGO partner, school leader, funder, children/parents); Micro → Movement
story threads (one EL's MIP adopted across units; one district model replicated statewide).

Pillar 3: Infrastructure to Scale Leadership Development (ELEVATE + AI)
Leadership at scale needs infrastructure that not only enables action, but also captures, connects,
and amplifies it.
What to communicate: infrastructure as a public good — ELEVATE + MItra are open, scalable
infrastructure adoptable across states, sectors (education, health, etc.) & organisations;
infrastructure enables leaders to act with agency (identify problems, take action, track
improvements → reduce dependency, increase ownership); from invisible efforts to visible impact
(micro-improvements captured & visible; local actions become part of a larger system narrative).
MItra: captures stories, discussions and insights through voice + multilingual AI; documents
grassroots action in real time.
Content ideas: byte-sized informational posts on technology's role; blog series on MItra / thought
leadership + ELEVATE adoption stories; impact stories/use cases; posts on how technology powers the
Shikshagraha movement.

Target Audience:
- Samaaj: Education NGOs & CSOs, nationally and globally (including ed techs)
- Sarkaar: stakeholders in national and state education departments and institutions — MoE, NCERT,
  SCERTs, DIETs
- Bazaar: tech service providers; funding ecosystem — philanthropists and HNIs, corporates

- Team ShikshaLokam

Metrics: Brand survey score.

=== TAB: Tab 2 (Channel plan) ===

Owned Media:
1. Social Media (LI, X, YT) — increased awareness & continuous engagement. Indicators: followership,
   engagement rates, user behaviour, co-created pieces, clicks/signups, brand survey, partnerships
   triggered, website traffic.
2. Newsletter — informed & engaged audience. Quarterly Bit & Bytes + themed emailers + dialogue
   emailers. Indicators: subscribers, open/click/response rate, reduced unsubscriptions.
3. Website — go-to trusted place; updated always, regular UI/UX enhancement for global appeal.
   Indicators: signups, traffic/clicks, shoutouts/references.

Earned Media:
4. Print/online media coverage — increased awareness. Indicators: pieces published (PR agency),
   organic features, website + social traffic.
5. Awards & recognitions — per year: 1 global award, 1 national award. Indicators: organic features,
   shoutouts/references.
6. Work showcase opportunities — target 2.

Projects:
7. Dialogues (InvokED, Shiksha Samvad, etc.) — InvokED once a year, others as required. Indicators:
   sign-ups, positive feedback, co-creators, post-dialogue collaborations.
8. Support to other teams — ensure all touchpoints are brand & messaging aligned.

Yearly success indicators: Brand Survey; # organisations partnered for advocacy nationally &
globally; mentions on partners'/co-builders' channels; referrals/tags by partners on social media.
