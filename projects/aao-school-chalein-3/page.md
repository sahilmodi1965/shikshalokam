# Aao School Chalein 3.0 — poster (community mobilization)

Poster to **rally the community to bring every child back to school**. Made for the "Claude Code → Canva"
pipeline: brain writes the brief + paste-ready prompt (+ preview); paste into Canva Magic Design.

> **⚠️ House rule (see [[design-consistency-real-photos]]):** ShikshaLokam designs use **real
> photographs, never illustrations/graphics**, and must match our existing posters. This poster was
> **reworked to a real-photo direction on 2026-07-03** (v1 used an illustration — off-brand). Exact
> palette / type / grid still to be **validated against our Drive posters** once located.

## Brief (locked with Ayush, 2026-07-03)
- **Language:** English (Hindi only in the campaign name, *Aao School Chalein 3.0*).
- **Type:** Community mobilization — an emotional rallying poster, no fixed event date; CTA = join the drive.
- **What the brain knows:** *Aao School Chalein* is a **Haryana** initiative that **re-enrolled dropout
  students** through **community-led, door-to-door campaigns**. **3.0** = third edition.
- **Feel:** warm, human, hopeful — a movement, not charity. **Photo-led**, framed in the brand.

## Locked copy (must appear)
- **Kicker (small, top):** A community drive to bring every child back to school
- **Campaign title (hero lockup):** **Aao School Chalein 3.0**
- **Headline (emotional):** **Every child back to school — one door at a time.**
- **Body:** A community-led, door-to-door drive to find the children who've slipped out of school —
  and walk them back to the classroom. Third year running. Every knock counts.
- **CTA:** **Join the drive →** *(+ how to join — contact/link)*
- **Footer:** space for logos (ShikshaLokam · partners)

## Visual direction (REAL PHOTO — house rule)
**Hero = a real photograph**, not an illustration: a warm, real photo of the campaign in action —
children and community members walking to school together, or a door-to-door enrolment moment. Real
faces, natural daylight, an Indian community setting; **hopeful and dignified, never pitying**. Full-bleed
photo up top, a solid **brand-colour band** below carrying the text, with a brand accent for the CTA —
colours from the **ShikshaLokam 2.0 Brand Kit** in Canva, not invented. **Avoid:** illustrations, cartoons, clip-art, abstract graphics, staged/charity-style imagery.
*(Final image must be a real ShikshaLokam photograph, ideally from a past Aao School Chalein drive.)*

## Canva AI prompt — paste into Magic Design / "Create a Design"
```
Create a 1:1 square poster (1080x1080) for a community campaign called "Aao School Chalein 3.0" (a Hindi name meaning "Come, let's go to school") — a call to bring every child back to school.

IMPORTANT: use a REAL PHOTOGRAPH, not an illustration or graphic. Photo: a warm, real photograph of children and community members walking to school together (or a door-to-door enrolment moment) — real faces, natural daylight, an Indian community setting, hopeful and dignified. Do NOT use illustrations, cartoons, clip-art, or abstract graphics. Do NOT depict children as sad or pitiable — show hope and collective action.

Layout: a full-bleed hero PHOTOGRAPH across the top ~60% of the poster; below it, a solid deep-indigo band holding the text.
- Small kicker over the photo (top): A community drive to bring every child back to school
- Campaign title, large, over the photo's lower edge on a subtle dark gradient: Aao School Chalein 3.0
- Headline in the indigo band (white): Every child back to school — one door at a time.
- Supporting line in the band: A community-led, door-to-door drive to find the children who've slipped out of school — and walk them back to the classroom. Third year running. Every knock counts.
- Call to action (prominent, warm gold/amber button): Join the drive
- Footer of the band: leave space for two logos

Colours: apply the ShikshaLokam 2.0 Brand Kit in Canva — use ONLY our official 2.0 palette for the band, buttons, accents and text. Do not invent colours.
Typography: clean modern sans-serif, strong bold headline, clear visual hierarchy.
Overall: photo-led, warm, hopeful, trustworthy — a real moment from the community, framed in the ShikshaLokam brand. Not an illustration.
```
