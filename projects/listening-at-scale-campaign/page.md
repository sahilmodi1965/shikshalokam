# Comms campaign plan — "One Question. Every Voice."

**Scope: what the communications team publishes.** Collection, consent and fieldwork sit with the
field and product teams — they're a *dependency* named here, never a task in this plan.

## The four constraints this plan is built around
1. **Few voices reach us, and unevenly.** No weekly-voice promise. When the field hands us one that's
   cleared, it leads that week. Otherwise the week runs on other pillars.
2. **What we receive is audio. No video exists.** Every voice format is built for audio — audiogram,
   transcript card, photograph. Nothing assumes footage.
3. **No public report, no public numbers.** The million is an **internal target for February**. If
   verified by then, it's said at InvokED — not before, and never as a milestone along the way.
4. **InvokED 6.0 is much bigger than listening.** Listening is one thread there. We introduce the
   idea to the ecosystem and hand it to the stage; we don't act like we own the event.

## The build order
**Meaning → the need to listen → the few voices we're given → the reveal at InvokED.**
Most of the run is the case for listening, carried by perspectives and legitimate information.

# 1 · The content rule — nothing empty

**Every post carries either a named perspective or a citable fact. Neither, it doesn't ship.**

## The five content pillars
| Pillar | What it is | Where comms gets it | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Evidence** | Cited information about future readiness and the gap | Sourced research filed in `wiki/sources/` — policy frameworks, national surveys, credible institutional studies. Nothing uncited, nothing ours | ~30% |
| **Perspective** | A named person's opinion, in their own words | SL leadership · partner leaders · funders · experts · headteachers we can reach directly. **Comms commissions these** — it's the pillar we fully control | ~30% |
| **Practice** | What is verifiably being done | Bihar MoU · chaupals and Samvaads · partner programmes · MItra's build story | ~15% |
| **Voice** | Audio the field passes to us, already consented | **Handed over, not gathered.** No quota. When one arrives cleared, it leads | ~15%, uneven |
| **Provocation** | The question itself, made participable | Polls · prompts · invitations to answer publicly | ~10% |

> ⚠️ **The evidence pillar is empty today.** Before Phase 1 goes live, run a research sweep and file
> each finding into `wiki/sources/` with its origin. **A statistic that isn't in the brain doesn't go
> in a post.** This is comms' own biggest unblock — a third of the campaign rests on it.

## The question the campaign puts into the world
1. **What does it mean to be future-ready?** — in your words, not a policy's.
2. **Is your child / are your students actually ready?** — honestly.
3. **What will it take?** — what has to change, and who has to do it.

Comms puts it to **leaders, partners and experts publicly**, and publishes what the field brings back
from children, teachers, parents and communities.

## What comms needs from the field team (dependency, not scope)
- Voices **already consented and cleared** for public use, with name, place and language.
- **A photograph** with each voice — they can photograph even where they can't film.
- A rough transcript or gist in the original language, so we can translate and subtitle.
- Nothing publishes without documented consent. That's the field team's sign-off, and we hold to it.

# 2 · LinkedIn (primary)

**3 org posts/week + 1 leader post/week.** Built on pillars comms can always fill, so a month with
no new voice still produces a full, substantive week.

| Format | What runs in it | Pillar | Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Document carousel (PDF)** | The case for listening in chapters · what the evidence says · what a chaupal actually is · the three questions explained | Evidence, Practice | 1 / week |
| **Text-only post** | Leaders and partners answering the question themselves. No graphic — reads as sincere, costs nothing | Perspective | 1 / week |
| **Audiogram** *(the voice format)* | The audio we're given, waveform animating, transcript on screen, translation below | Voice | when one arrives |
| **Quote card + photograph** | A line from a named person over a real field photograph. Never an illustration; never a stock face implying it's the speaker | Perspective, Voice | 1 / week |
| **Single image + long caption** | Field photography carrying an evidence or practice point | Evidence, Practice | fortnightly |
| **Poll** | "Who should decide what future-ready means?" — the question, made clickable | Provocation | 1 / month |
| **Article (long-form)** | The opening byline · partner bylines · expert guest pieces | Perspective | 1 / month |
| **LinkedIn Newsletter** | "Why We Listen" — pushes to every subscriber | Evidence, Perspective | monthly |
| **LinkedIn Live** | Launch conversation · a partner conversation · the InvokED lead-in | Perspective | 1 / phase |
| **Horizontal video 2–4 min** | Launch film built from photographs, type and audio — no interview footage required · MItra demo · InvokED lead-in | Evidence, Practice | 1 / phase |
| **Multi-image post** | "What we heard this month" — several transcript cards in one scroll, when several have come through | Voice | as available |
| **Repost with commentary** | Leadership, team and partners resharing with their own sentence | all | every major post |
| **First comment · mentions · banner · Featured** | CTA in comment one · tag partners and institutions · campaign banner · Featured pins the question | — | ongoing |

## Weekly rhythm — built so it never runs dry
- **Tue — Evidence.** A carousel or image post carrying one sourced, citable fact.
- **Thu — Perspective.** A named person's answer: our leader, a partner, an expert.
- **Sat — Practice or Voice.** What's verifiably happening — or, when one has come through, an
  audiogram. The voice always outranks the practice post.
- **Weekly:** one leader posts from their own profile. CTA in first comment. Team reshares within an
  hour, leaders within a day.

## How an audio-only voice becomes a post
- **Audiogram** — the audio, animated waveform, transcript on screen, translation underneath.
- **Transcript card** — the line set in type over a real photograph, name and place attributed.
- **Never** an illustration, an avatar, or a stock face standing in for a real speaker.

# 3 · WhatsApp (secondary — distribution)

Where the field audience is. **Comms uses it to publish, not to collect** — anything inbound is the
field team's to handle.

| Surface | What comms runs in it | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| **Channel** | The field-facing home — audio clips, images, polls, links | 2 / week |
| **Partner & community groups** | SLC / YLC / WLC, Mantra networks, HM groups — forwardable cards. Never a link dump | 2 / week |
| **Status** | Behind the scenes from chaupals, Samvaads and campaign moments | bursts |
| **Broadcast lists** | Opted-in field teams and headteachers — the week's piece | weekly |
| **Forwardable creatives** | 1:1 + 9:16, hard subtitles, small file size, type large enough for a cheap phone | every drop |
| **PDF one-pager · sticker pack** | For field teams' own groups; the three questions as stickers | Ph. 1 · Ph. 2 |

**Two cuts always:** 1:1 or 16:9 for LinkedIn, 9:16 and lighter for WhatsApp. Nothing posts to one
platform without its counterpart cut existing.

# 4 · Partner engine — reach *and* content supply

Partners solve two comms problems: distribution, and **supply**. When voices are thin, partner
perspectives keep the campaign full and credible.

| Partner | What they bring | The comms ask |
|---|---|---|
| **Mantra4Change** *(anchor)* | Widest channels, deepest reach. **Anuja** runs the LS master sheet; **Pratik Jain** owns key message and archetype | Run the campaign on their channels · **two perspective posts a month from named leaders** · a Takeover Week · one byline · joint InvokED presence |
| **SLC / YLC / WLC networks** | School leaders, youth leaders, women leaders | Their leaders answer the question publicly · carry the week's piece into their groups |
| **State partners — Bihar, Nagaland, Karnataka** | Institutional credibility no figure could buy | Named mentions · official quotes · clearance to use existing programme photography |
| **Funders, advisors, experts** | Ecosystem weight, and citable thinking | Answer the question publicly · guest bylines · point us to research worth filing |
| **Schools & headteachers** | Named perspective from the ground | A written or recorded answer · reshare their own school's post |

## The Partner Pack — build first, it unlocks everything
Campaign brief on a page · the three-questions card · **co-brandable templates** (carousel, quote
card, audiogram end-slate) · **ready-to-post captions**, three per format, editable, EN/HI/KN ·
hashtag and tagging protocol · the calendar of which weeks are theirs.

## Four mechanics that make partners actually post
- **Perspective commitments** — each anchor partner commits to a named number of leader posts per
  month. The content-supply deal, agreed up front. It's what makes Thursdays reliable.
- **Takeover Weeks** — a partner owns the week: their content, their channels, their framing; we reshare.
- **Joint LinkedIn Live** — SL + Mantra + a state partner.
- **The credit rule** — anything a partner contributes is published crediting them, every time.

Running it: one partner WhatsApp group (comms leads), assets dropped the day before each push, and a
30-minute monthly huddle.

| Months | Phase | What the ecosystem sees | Voices |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Aug–Sep** | **Why listening matters** | The case, built on evidence and perspective. Opening byline · launch film · poll · Live #1 · leaders answering publicly · newsletter begins | none published |

| **Dec–Jan** | **What we're beginning to hear** | Voices lead whenever one comes through. Framed honestly — *"here's one we heard this month"* — never a tally. Themes emerge in carousels | as they arrive |
| **Feb** | **InvokED 6.0** | Listening takes its place as **one thread** at the event. Whatever is true by then — including the number, if verified — is said there, not before | the reveal |

**Dropped:** the public Listening Report. The material won't support it, and a thin report would
damage the idea more than no report. If a synthesis is warranted by January, it's a carousel series.
