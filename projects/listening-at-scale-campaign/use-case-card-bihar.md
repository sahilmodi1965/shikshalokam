## MItra in Bihar
### Listening at scale, now formalised with a state government

**Where:** The **Department of Education, Government of Bihar** — a statewide MoU, signed at zero cost
to the state, alongside six fellow education organisations.

**What MItra does here:**
- SL's role under the MoU is to **connect the voices of parents, teachers and students to the system
  through an AI-based framework** — listening at scale, built into how the state runs.
- MItra is the tool that captures those voices: voice-enabled, multilingual, conversational.
  *"Not a survey. Not a form. A companion."*
- The people closest to each child become partners in learning — heard, not surveyed.

**Why it matters:**
- This is the first time listening at scale is **formalised with a state department** — it moves from
  a good idea to public infrastructure.
- It's a **collective effort** (seven organisations, one department), at **zero cost to the state** —
  proof the model scales without a price tag.
- It plugs Bihar's schools into the same listening movement already at **32,742 chaupals · 8.79 lakh
  members · 79,148 challenges surfaced** *(source: [[../../wiki/sources/listening-at-scale-corpus-2026]])*
  — the road toward one million voices.
