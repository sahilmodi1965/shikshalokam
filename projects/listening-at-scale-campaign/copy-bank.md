# Copy bank — drafted, not approved

Every piece below is a first draft awaiting Sonal. `[square brackets]` = a real detail to fill once
the voice or source is in hand.

**Four rules govern everything here:**
- **Nothing empty** — every post carries a named perspective or a citable fact, or it doesn't ship.
- **No public numbers** — the million is an internal February target, never a claim we make now.
- **Audio only** — what reaches us is audio. No video footage exists; nothing implies it does.
- **Comms scope** — we publish. Collection, consent and fieldwork sit with the field team; voices
  reach us already cleared.

## LinkedIn — launch post *(Phase 1 · org page + Shibulal sir)*

> India has decided where it wants to be by 2047.
>
> It has not decided who gets to say what "ready" means.
>
> Right now that answer is being written in rooms that don't include the teacher who knows the child
> by name, the mother who wakes her at five, the headteacher who keeps the school open when nobody
> is watching.
>
> So we are asking them. Three questions, in their languages, across the country:
>
> What does future-ready mean to you?
> Is your child ready?
> What will it take?
>
> Every answer travels to the InvokED 6.0 stage this February.
>
> Speak, and you will be heard there. #OneQuestionEveryVoice

## LinkedIn — audiogram caption *(published when a voice lands — no fixed cadence)*

**Format:** real audio, animated waveform, transcript on screen, translation below. No video footage
exists and none is implied. A photograph of the speaker if we have one; otherwise transcript on type.

> [Name] teaches Class 4 in a government school in [district].
>
> We asked her what "future-ready" means.
>
> She didn't say coding. She didn't say English.
>
> "[her line, verbatim, in her language — transcript on screen, translation below]"
>
> This is her voice, not our summary of it. Sound on.
>
> #OneQuestionEveryVoice #ListeningAtScale

## LinkedIn — evidence post *(Tuesdays — one sourced fact, never ours)*

**Rule:** the figure or finding must already exist in `wiki/sources/` with its origin recorded. If
it isn't filed in the brain, it doesn't go in the post.

> [One sourced finding, stated plainly, with the source named in the line — e.g. "According to
> [source, year], …"]
>
> [Two or three sentences on what that means for the child in a government classroom.]
>
> This is why we're asking three questions of the people closest to those children — and publishing
> what they say.
>
> Source: [full citation]. #OneQuestionEveryVoice
