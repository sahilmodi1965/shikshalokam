# InvokED Impact Survey — campaign plan

**The job:** move 150–250 senior education leaders through a 5-minute MItra conversation, and turn
what they say into a report that lands **by 30 September 2026**.

**The real difficulty is not the survey.** It's the response rate. We need **20–25% of a cold alumni
base of ~800–1,000** — people who are senior, global and time-poor. A single announcement email
gets 3–8%. That maths is the reason this is a campaign and not a send.

> ⚠️ **Everything below is back-planned from 30 Sep. The field window is only four weeks
> (17 Aug – 13 Sep).** Every day lost in prep comes out of field, and field is what produces the
> sample. See §7 for the one dependency that can sink the whole thing.

---

## 2 · The positioning — three decisions that set the response rate

### 2a · Don't call it a survey
The word kills it with this audience. It signals *form*, *extraction*, *ten minutes I don't have*.
The PRD's own strength is that it **isn't** a form — it's a conversation with probing.

**Recommended name: "InvokED — What Happened Next."**
It's the actual question, it's about them, and it implies a story rather than a scale.

Alternates, if we want a different register:
- *"The InvokED Effect"* — punchier, more institutional, slightly more self-congratulatory.
- *"Five editions. What came of them?"* — most honest, most open to a hard answer.
- *"What did InvokED set in motion?"* — lifts the report's own opening block.

**In every asset, the format is the pitch:** *"Five minutes. A conversation, not a form. Voice or
text, whichever you prefer."*

### 2b · Give them something back — this is the gap in the PRD
The PRD is thorough on what the survey **takes** (28 metrics, per-conversation reports, Awards
leads) and silent on what the alumnus **gets**. For this audience that gap is the difference between
an 8% and a 25% response rate. Fix it with a **reciprocity ladder**, stated in the invite:

| Tier | The offer | Who gets it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Early access** to the InvokED Impact Report, before public release | Every respondent |
| 2 | **The Future Readiness read** — how 150–250 senior education leaders define a term everyone uses and nobody defines the same way | Every respondent |
| 3 | Your **action story featured**; named in the report; considered for a **Shikshagraha Award**; invited to an **InvokED Forum** | Opt-in |

Tier 2 is the strongest card we hold. **Q8 is not just our research question — it is a peer study
these people would genuinely want to be counted in.** Lead the invite with it, not with our impact
measurement. Nobody fills in a form so an organisation can prove its own value; people do contribute
to a study of their own field.

### 2c · Be honest that a "no" is welcome
The PRD's methodology note 1 says a No on Q4 is not an absence of impact. Say that *to the
respondent, in the invite*: **"If InvokED led to nothing for you, that's a genuinely useful answer
and we'd like it."** It removes the reason a non-collaborator would skip — and non-collaborators are
exactly who Q5 and the Forum lead-list need.

## 4 · The response maths (why four waves)

Working base ~900 reachable (assume 750–850 deliverable after list hygiene).

| Wave | Audience | Rate | Completions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — personal, named sender | 150 | 35% | ~52 |
| 2 — segment mail | 750 | 8% | ~60 |
| 3 — reminder + WhatsApp/LinkedIn | ~840 non-responders | 5% | ~42 |
| 4 — last call + phone top 50 | ~800 | 4% | ~32 |
| | | **Total** | **≈ 185** |

Lands inside the 150–250 band — **but only with all four waves and a genuinely personal Wave 1.**

**One blast alone: ~72. A clear miss.** Worth stating plainly to anyone who proposes it.

**Watch persona mix, not just the total.** 200 responses that are all civil-society practitioners is
a weaker report than 150 spread across funders, government, academia and edtech. The PRD says
"diversity = credibility." Track the mix weekly and target Wave 3/4 at the thin segments.

## 7 · Risks — one of these can sink it

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **MItra instance not live and tested by 14 Aug** | 🔴 **Critical path.** Every day of slip comes out of a 4-week field window | Confirm the product team's build date **this week**. If it can't be ready by 14 Aug, we either move the report date or fall back to a form — decide by 14 Aug, not later |
| **List quality** — do we have clean, current contact data for 800–1,000 people across five editions (2021–2026)? | 🔴 High | Audit in prep week. Registrant records from 1.0–2.0 will be the weakest. Report the real reachable base before Wave 1, not after |
| **Only one wave gets sent** | 🔴 High | Waves 3 and 4 are ~40% of the sample. Lock the dates now, with owners |
| **Persona skew** | 🟠 Medium | Weekly mix check; target thin segments in Waves 3–4 |
| **The 5-minute claim isn't true** | 🟠 Medium | Time the pilot honestly in prep week. If it runs 9 minutes, say 9. Overrunning a stated time with senior people costs future goodwill |
| **Awards outreach makes it feel transactional** | 🟠 Medium | Keep Awards as an opt-in Tier 3, at the end. Never in the invite's opening |
| **Low N makes percentages misleading** | 🟠 Medium | Below ~120, report counts and stories, not percentages. Decide the threshold before we see the data, not after |
| **Consent / PII** for named orgs, quotes, photos | 🟠 Medium | Explicit opt-in inside the conversation for naming and quoting. Confirm with the product team it's in the flow |

## 8 · Phase 2 — the report and its runway (sketch)

Delivered **30 Sep**. Then it works for four months:

- **Oct** — the credibility narrative goes public: the headline is the PRD's own line, *"InvokED is
  not a two-day event"* (the combined Q4+Q5 "any post-event effect" number). Action stories as
  standalone pieces.
- **Oct–Nov** — the **Future Readiness** piece: how 150–250 senior education leaders define it. This
  is the asset with a life beyond InvokED, and the one we promised respondents.
- **Nov–Dec** — **Forum & partnership leads** worked: the latent collaborations and stalled action
  from the Q4 No branch are a warm list, not a failure list.
- **Dec–Feb** — feeds **InvokED 6.0** (26–27 Feb 2027): the invite's proof paragraph, the stage
  narrative, Shikshagraha Awards nominations, and the ambassadors list.

Every one of these is bound by the PRD's five methodology notes — especially: **never report the Q4
No on its own**, and **never imply the perception shift is a measured before/after.** Those rules
travel with the data into every asset.
