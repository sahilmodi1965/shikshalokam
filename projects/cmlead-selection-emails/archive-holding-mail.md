# Earlier all-applicant holding mail

Sent before the shortlist was finalised. Superseded by the two mails on
[[cmlead-selection-emails]]. Archived here when the Doc's spare tabs were cleared.

Note: this mail said "all further communication will be shared only with shortlisted candidates".
The current Email 2 reverses that, deliberately.

---

From: State Project Director, Samagra Shiksha, Department of Education, Government of Meghalaya

Subject: Meghalaya CMLEAD Fellowship: an update on your application

Dear Applicant,

Thank you for applying to the Meghalaya CMLEAD Fellowship.

When we invited young professionals to join us in strengthening education in Meghalaya, we hoped the idea would find takers. It found 1,398 of them, from across India. That response has been humbling, and it says something about how many young people are willing to give their time to public education.

Screening applications of this volume with the care each one deserves has taken longer than we had planned. We are aware this has meant a longer wait than you would have liked, and we thank you for your patience.

The screening is now in its final stage. Shortlisted candidates will be contacted directly for interviews. Interviews will conclude by the last week of July 2026, and the 12 selected Fellows will be announced in the first week of August 2026.

Please note that all further communication regarding the selection process will be shared only with shortlisted candidates.

The Fellows selected will work inside our district education system, across nearly 8,000 government and aided schools: strengthening foundational learning, standing alongside teachers and school leaders, and turning policy into everyday classroom practice. It is demanding work, and it matters.

Whatever the outcome of your application, thank you for your interest in Meghalaya's children and their learning.

With warm regards,

[Name]
State Project Director, Samagra Shiksha
Department of Education, Government of Meghalaya
