# CMLEAD Fellowship, selection-outcome emails

Two emails. **45 of 1,398** shortlisted for Stage 3, the jury-led final interview, for **12** Fellow
positions. Both are in the voice of the **State Project Director, Samagra Shiksha**, following on
from the all-applicant update already sent.

- **Status:** review-ready. Nothing sent, nothing in Drive.
- **Blocking:** the interview date, time and mode are not in the brain. Every `[[...]]` below is a
  real gap Sonal has to fill before Email 1 can go.

## Email 1, to the 45 shortlisted

**From:** State Project Director, Samagra Shiksha, Department of Education, Government of Meghalaya
**Subject:** Meghalaya CMLEAD Fellowship: invitation to the final interview

Dear [[Candidate name]],

Thank you for the time and thought you gave to your application to the Meghalaya CMLEAD Fellowship.

We received 1,398 applications from across India. After screening on eligibility, experience and the
written assignment, **45 candidates have been shortlisted for the final stage of selection.** You are
one of them.

Your interview with the selection panel is scheduled as follows.

> **Date:** [[date]]
> **Time:** [[time]] (please join or arrive [[15]] minutes early)
> **Mode:** [[in person at <venue, Shillong> / online, joining link below]]
> **Duration:** approximately [[30]] minutes

The panel is chaired by the Commissioner and Secretary, Department of Education, or the State Project
Director, Samagra Shiksha, and includes officials from DSEL, SSA-SEMAM, DERT, a District School
Education Officer, and a representative of our knowledge partner, Mantra4Change.

The conversation will cover how you approach problems, how you communicate, your readiness for
field work in the districts, and what draws you to public service. You do not need to prepare a
presentation. Speak to your own experience and to what you wrote in your application.

Please keep the following at hand: [[proof of age, degree certificates, experience letters,
photo identity]].

Kindly confirm your attendance by replying to this email by [[confirmation date]]. If the time given
does not work for you, write to [[contact name, email, phone]] and we will do our best to
accommodate a change.

The 12 selected Fellows will be announced in the first week of August 2026.

We look forward to speaking with you.

With warm regards,

[[SPD Name]]
State Project Director, Samagra Shiksha
Department of Education, Government of Meghalaya

## Email 2, to those not shortlisted

**From:** State Project Director, Samagra Shiksha, Department of Education, Government of Meghalaya
**Subject:** Meghalaya CMLEAD Fellowship: an update on your application

Dear [[Candidate name / Applicant]],

Thank you for applying to the Meghalaya CMLEAD Fellowship, and for the care you gave to your
statement of purpose and your written assignment.

We received 1,398 applications from across India for 12 Fellow positions. After screening on
eligibility, experience and the assignment, 45 candidates have been shortlisted for the final
interview. Your application has not been taken forward to that stage.

We know how little that sentence explains, so let us be plain about it. With twelve places and
1,398 applications, the great majority of strong, capable people who applied could not be
shortlisted. This outcome says something about the number of places we have. It does not say
something about your ability or your intent.

What stayed with us is that 1,398 people were willing to spend a year of their working lives inside
government schools in Meghalaya, in districts that are remote and hard to reach, for children most
of the country never hears about. That is not a small thing, and it is not something we take for
granted.

Meghalaya's education system needs far more people than one Fellowship can hold. We are keeping your
application on record, and if further opportunities open with the Department or with our partners,
we will be glad for you to consider them.

Wherever you go next, we hope it keeps you close to classrooms. Thank you for turning your attention
to Meghalaya's children and their learning.

With warm regards,

[[SPD Name]]
State Project Director, Samagra Shiksha
Department of Education, Government of Meghalaya

## ⚠️ Decisions and gaps before these go out

**Blocking Email 1**
1. **Date, time, mode, venue or joining link.** Not in the brain. If interviews are staggered across
   days and slots, this needs to be a **mail merge** with a per-candidate slot, not one broadcast.
2. **Contact point for rescheduling** (name, email, phone).
3. **Documents to carry.** The list above is my assumption; confirm what the panel actually wants.
4. **Interview duration** and how early to arrive or join.

**Blocking both**
5. **The SPD's name.** Still a placeholder, carried over from the earlier mail. This is the second
   piece of candidate-facing content held up by it.

**Judgement calls Sonal should confirm**
6. **The earlier mail said only shortlisted candidates would be contacted.** Email 2 reverses that.
   It is a better decision and worth making, but it is a change of position, so it should be a
   conscious one.
7. **No second cohort is promised.** Year 2 is a July/August 2027 decision, so Email 2 says
   "if further opportunities open", never "next year". Do not let this soften into a promise.
8. **The rejection names the numbers** (1,398, 45, 12). It is more respectful than a vague no, and
   it makes the odds legible. Say the word if the Department would rather not publish the shortlist
   count.
9. **"Not selected" is 1,353 people.** Send as individual mails or bcc, never a visible recipient
   list.
