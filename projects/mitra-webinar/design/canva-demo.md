# Showcase — "Claude Code → Canva" MItra poster (live demo)

**The story we're showing:** you talk to the brain (Claude Code) → it produces a brand-true, on-voice
design prompt (+ a preview of the target) → you paste it into Canva → Canva renders the poster live →
a light brand tweak, export. The value: the *thinking* (message, hierarchy, brand, what NOT to show)
is done by the brain; Canva does the rendering.

## Run-sheet (≈4 min)
1. **Set up the "before".** Open Canva → *Create a design* → *Whiteboard/Doc* isn't it; use
   **"Create a design" → search "1080×1080"** (or *Magic Design* with a text prompt). Have the brand
   colours handy (below) or a Brand Kit loaded.
2. **The ask (on screen).** In Claude Code: *"make the MItra webinar poster prompt."* The brain returns
   the prompt below — narrate that it already knows the campaign, the voice, the impact numbers, and
   deliberately keeps MItra tiny.
3. **Show the preview.** Open `design/poster-mockup.html` in a browser — "this is the target the prompt
   is aiming at." (Layout, palette, voices→insight visual.)
4. **Paste into Canva.** Copy the prompt → Canva **Magic Design** (or *Magic Media → Design*) → paste →
   generate. Pick the closest result.
5. **Brand tweak, live.** Swap fonts to the brand sans, drop the two logos into the footer, replace the
   QR placeholder, confirm the palette. Export PNG/PDF.
6. **Close the loop.** "The brain also saved this prompt + preview in the project, so anyone on the team
   can regenerate or restyle it." (It's in `projects/mitra-webinar/`.)

## The paste-ready prompt (copy this whole block into Canva)
> Design a premium 1:1 square poster (1080×1080) for a ShikshaLokam webinar. The hero of the poster is
> the act of listening to India's communities at scale — the voices of parents, teachers, and young
> leaders finally being heard. This is NOT a product launch for a tool; a platform called MItra is only
> HOW the listening happens and should appear only once, small, in the top-left eyebrow.
>
> Theme: communities being truly heard, at scale.
>
> Visual concept: across the left and centre, a warm, human chorus of many soft voice waveforms —
> plural, organic, alive — representing real community members speaking in their own words. Toward the
> right, these gently resolve into a small, calm cluster of structured insight (a few clean dots or
> shapes) — the voices are heard and understood, not lost. Keep the visual weight on the human voices
> and the act of listening; the insight side stays quiet and secondary. Do not make it look like an
> analytics dashboard or a SaaS product. Do not use survey forms, clipboards, checkboxes, phones,
> robots, AI heads, microphones, or chatbot graphics.
>
> Aesthetic: modern civic-tech, generous whitespace, premium editorial typography, subtle abstract
> geometric elements, warm and human, clean visual hierarchy.
>
> Layout:
> - Top-left eyebrow: MItra Webinar · for ecosystem partners
> - Hero headline (large, bold): India's communities are speaking. Are we listening?
> - Small tagline under the hero: Not a survey. Not a form. A companion.
> - Supporting line: Listening to India's communities, at scale.
> - Details row: 17 July 2026 · 4:00–5:00 PM IST · Online (Zoom)   [SAMPLE — replace with final]
> - Impact strip along the bottom: 32,742 chaupals · 8.79 lakh voices · 79,148 challenges surfaced
> - Bottom-right: a Register button and a square placeholder for a QR code
> - Footer: leave space for two logos
>
> Colours (ShikshaLokam 2.0): deep indigo, royal purple, bright violet, teal accents, white, warm
> off-white, dark slate. Use gradients sparingly.
> Typography: modern sans-serif, strong hierarchy, large bold headline, elegant spacing, minimal
> decoration.
> Overall feeling: it should immediately read as being about truly listening to communities — hearing
> the people closest to every child, at scale. Sophisticated, trustworthy, deeply human, with plenty of
> breathing room.

## Talking points (why this matters)
- **Brand-safe by default** — the brain carries the palette, voice, and the "keep MItra small" call, so
  the design starts on-brand, not from a blank prompt.
- **Message before decoration** — the hero, hierarchy, and what to *avoid* (dashboards, robots, forms)
  are decided by the brief, not left to luck.
- **Reusable** — the prompt + preview live in the project; restyle or resize (story, banner) in seconds.
