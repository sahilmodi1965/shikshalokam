Collaborative Philanthropy

A new page pulling together what we already hold on funding and funders — from the SL 2.0 strategy note, the InvokED corpus, and the external evidence. Before it becomes something drafts cite from, it needs a read.

Awaiting confirmation Drafted by Ayush 4 Aug 2026 Lives at wiki/concepts/collaborative-philanthropy.md

The thesis it lands on

Philanthropy that funds isolated projects buys outputs. Philanthropy that funds leadership, coherence, and long-term institutional capacity — alongside government, community, markets and media — builds a system that keeps improving after the grant ends.

The differentiator the material gave us: in both Punjab and Nagaland, the funder sits inside the collective with a role, not outside it with a reporting requirement. That line is now explicit on the page.

What the page asserts

  • ✓ Fund Orchestration is one of six capabilities SL 2.0 must add (HNIs + institutions + philanthropies), with a standing Funding Orchestration Team. SL 2.0 Strategy Note, Jul 2025
  • ✓ Fund is one of the 10 verbs of the movement — level with Amplify, Convene, Orchestrate, Mentor. Money is one contribution among ten, not the senior one. SL 2.0 Strategy Note, Jul 2025
  • ✓ Funders have named roles — Strategic Partners (Axis Bank, AWS, Societal Thinking), Anchor Partners (Commutiny), Momentum Partners (Vidhya Vidhai, Jyothi Mahila Samakhya). Better vocabulary than "donor". SL 2.0 Strategy Note, ecosystem role map
  • ✓ "Capital beyond money" — trust, networks, relationships, knowledge, governance, shared ownership. From the InvokED 5.0 Brain Date "Beyond Fundraising — Orchestrating Capital". InvokED 5.0 proceedings + approved session caption
  • ✓ The three Roundtable questions we already put to funders, carried verbatim: isolated projects → capacity; funders as bridges across Sarkaar/Samaaj/Bazaar/ Sanchar; conditions for a movement to thrive. Strategic Partners Roundtable invite, S.D. Shibulal
  • ✓ The evidence — Punjab Education Collective moved 13th → 1st in two years, funded by Deloitte and A.T.E. Chandra Foundation; Nagaland PBL funded by Education Above All. Both corroborated by the WEF report. Brazil / Lemann Summit stories + WEF The Future is Collective
