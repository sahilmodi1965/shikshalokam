# InvokED 5.0 session teaser — anatomy + voice (2026)

The **Friday** slot: a teaser for an **InvokED 5.0 session already on YouTube**, driving views. Distinct
from the Wednesday Studio caption ([[invoked-studio-caption-2026]]) — this one is **intellectual /
systems-change**, features **multiple expert speakers**, and opens on a **provocative question**.
Captured from 4 live posts. Going forward the brain drafts these directly.

## Anatomy (top → bottom)
1. **Hook** — a sharp rhetorical/"what-if" **question**, or a framing statement about the session:
   - *"How do we move from conversation to collective action?"*
   - *"What if the most valuable capital for systems change isn't money—but trust?"*
   - *"The Brain Date on Evidence in Motion – Collectivising Knowledge challenged conventional ideas of evidence, data, and expertise."*
2. **Session + speakers** — `At InvokED 5.0, <Name>(<Role> at <Org>), <Name>(<Role> at <Org>), … and
   <Name>(<Role> at <Org>) explored / came together to reflect on <the question/topic>.` **Every
   speaker named with role @ org**, in parentheses, comma-separated (last joined with "and").
3. **Insight paragraph** — 1–2 sentences distilling the systems-change takeaway ("The conversation
   highlighted that lasting systems change is driven not just by policies and programs, but by
   people…"). House vocabulary: trust · relationships · shared purpose · collaboration across
   differences · lived experience · community voices · orchestration.
4. **CTA** — `🔴 Watch the full session here:` (or `on YouTube:`) + the lnkd.in link.
5. **Hashtags** — `#InvokED2026` + `#SystemsChange` almost always; plus 2–4 topic tags
   (#Shikshagraha #CollectiveLeadership #Collaboration #Philanthropy #Education #CollectiveKnowledge).
6. **Optional extras:** a second CTA (`🟣` for a nominations/related link), and **@-tagging** the
   people/orgs involved (seen on the Awards post: Pratik Jain · Shwetha Kamath · Anish Saha · Aquib
   Rizwan · Vinaya Kurtkoti).

## Voice rules this exemplifies
- **Lead with a question** the session answers (or a crisp "challenged/celebrated" statement).
- **Name every speaker** with role @ org — this is a credibility post; the line-up *is* the draw.
- **One insight, not a summary** — distil the systems-change idea, don't recap the agenda.
- **Systems-change register:** trust, relationships, shared purpose, orchestration, collective — not
  "learnings" or "takeaways."
- **Fixed CTA + tag block:** 🔴 Watch → #InvokED2026 #SystemsChange (+topic). 🟣 for a second link.
- Leave `[link]` as a placeholder until the real YouTube/lnkd.in URL is supplied.
