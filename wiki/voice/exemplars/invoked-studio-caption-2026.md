# InvokED Studio caption — anatomy + voice (2026)

The reusable format for **InvokED Studio "Up Close & Personal with GenNext"** episode captions —
the Wednesday slot on [[../../../projects/captions/page]]. Student hosts interview an education
leader; each episode gets one social post. Captured from Ayush's caption chat (real outputs for
S03 EP09–EP14). **Going forward the brain drafts these directly — no ChatGPT step.**

## Anatomy (top → bottom)

1. **Opener (bold):** `**Episode <N> of Up Close & Personal with GenNext is out.**`
2. **Guest + host lockup:** **Guest Name** *(Title, Organisation)* joins [student / GenNext] hosts
   **Host 1** and **Host 2** [from **Org**, e.g. Mantra4Change] for a **heartfelt conversation on
   <2–3 themes: leadership, learning, self-belief…>**.
3. **Body (1–2 short paragraphs):** the "From … to …" move — open on the show's nostalgia segment
   (*School Chalam*: report cards, notebooks, lunch breaks, school memories) → pivot to the deeper
   reflections. Distil what the guest actually said, in the house themes (below). Refer to the guest
   by first name.
4. **CTAs (two, colour-dot emoji):**
   `🔴 **Watch the full episode here:** [YouTube link]`
   `🟢 **Listen on Spotify:** [Spotify link]`
5. **Hashtags (fixed stack):** `#CandidConversation #InvokED2026 #GlobalDialogue`

## Voice rules this exemplifies
- **Warm, plainspoken, "heartfelt."** Never corporate. The word *heartfelt* recurs.
- **Nostalgia → depth.** Always open the body on the *School Chalam* memory beat before the ideas.
- **House thesis on leadership:** *"not about authority or titles"* — it's initiative, care, empathy,
  responsibility, creating opportunities, believing in potential, asking questions, grassroots action.
- **Recurring vocabulary:** curiosity · confidence · collective action · education equity · potential ·
  "believe in themselves" · leaders who create more leaders.
- **Name everyone** — guest (with title + org) and both student hosts (with org if given).
- **Fixed CTA + hashtag block** — 🔴 YouTube, 🟢 Spotify, then the 3-tag stack. Leave `[Insert … Link]`
  placeholders until the real links are supplied.
- Guest referred to by **first name** in the body.

## Secondary output the same source produces — quote cards / thumbnails
The chat also generates **pull-quote lines** for the thumbnail + quote cards. Rule (important): use the
guest's **actual words** (or faithful paraphrase), never invented quotes. Keep thumbnail lines **≤ 8–10
words**. Examples: *"The marks really do not convey what a student is really capable of."* ·
*"A true leader creates more leaders."*
