# ShikshaLokam Communications Strategy (2026) — the SL-org comms bible

**Source:** Google Doc shared by Sonal, 2026-07-10 (canonical SL comms strategy). Raw extract: [[../../raw/2026-07-10]].
**Why this matters:** freshest authoritative SL-org language — supersedes/updates parts of the
[[shikshalokam-2-strategy-note-jul2025]] (Jul 2025) for **current comms**. Where the two differ on
"how SL says it today," this file wins; the strategy note remains canonical for the 1.0→2.0 thesis,
SFPI origin, ecosystem-role map and internal team structure.

## Mission statement (verbatim — current)

> *"Enabling and amplifying leadership development opportunities for individuals and institutions engaged in K-12 education systems."*

Note the shift from the Jul-2025 line (*"Enabling leadership development across India's K-12 education
system"*) → now **"enabling AND amplifying … for individuals AND institutions."** Target unchanged:
empower **4.5M education leaders by 2030** (→ 54M micro-improvements/year at 1/leader/month).

## The Anchor (the one core idea)

> *"ShikshaLokam enables education leadership by restoring their agency — the ability of individuals to drive continuous micro-improvements in their context."*

**Restoring agency → continuous improvement → systemic transformation** is the load-bearing chain.
Leadership = **the Act of Improvement** (not title): teachers, principals, officials, parents, youth,
community members. See [[leadership]].

## The continuous-improvement loop (from shikshalokam.org) — Sense → Make Sense → Learn → Improve

Canonical framework replacing the older I-HAVE/I-CAN/I-WISH framing for comms:

1. **Sense** — listen, observe and assess the local context (data, observation, feedback).
2. **Make Sense** — analyse data + peer dialogue to prioritise and design improvements.
3. **Learn** — build knowledge/skills via courses, mentorship, communities of practice.
4. **Improve** — plan and lead small, goal-oriented **micro-improvements** that move student outcomes.

> I-HAVE / I-CAN / I-WISH ([[shikshalokam-2-strategy-note-jul2025]]) is the *agency-psychology* framing;
> Sense/Make Sense/Learn/Improve is the *practice-cycle* framing. Both are SL's — use the loop for
> "how leaders work," I-HAVE/I-CAN/I-WISH for "how agency grows."

## The infrastructure: ELEVATE + MItra

- **ELEVATE** — open, scalable **digital public infrastructure (DPI)**; adoptable across states,
  sectors (education, health…) and organisations. Institutionalised federally via Vidya Amrit + DIKSHA.
- **MItra** — **voice-first, multilingual AI** that captures grassroots stories, discussions and
  insights and **documents on-ground action in real time** — turning invisible micro-improvements into
  visible, connected, system-level intelligence. See [[mitra-listening-at-scale-part1-2026]].

## The three communication goals (verbatim)

1. **Amplify the imperative role of education leadership** in improving public schools (restoring
   agency → continuous improvement → systemic transformation).
2. **Strengthen SL's identity as the Shikshagraha movement builder** — catalyse and legitimise
   collective action by showing how leadership missions & partnerships are emerging across states,
   districts and communities; articulate SL's role in **government system orchestration** + **network
   catalysis**.
3. **Amplify ELEVATE (DPI) + MItra (AI)** as the tools to scale leadership development.

## The three narrative pillars

1. **Leadership with Agency in Action (across geographies)** — make agency visible through real
   micro-improvements; ed-leader portraits, coffee-table book, data-driven insights.
2. **Shikshagraha as a Movement in Action** — collective action across Samaaj/Sarkaar/Bazaar/Sanchaar;
   bright spots, movement data (# leaders, improvements, states/districts, partners), convening→action
   stories (InvokED/Shiksha Samvad → partnerships → pilots → programs → Mission SOAR), Voices from the
   Movement, Micro→Movement threads.
3. **Infrastructure to Scale Leadership Development (ELEVATE + AI)** — infrastructure as a public good;
   enables action + captures/connects/amplifies it; invisible efforts → visible impact via MItra.

## Target audience (Samaaj / Sarkaar / Bazaar map)

- **Samaaj** — education NGOs & CSOs, national + **global** (incl. ed-techs).
- **Sarkaar** — national + state education departments & institutions: **MoE, NCERT, SCERTs, DIETs**.
- **Bazaar** — tech service providers; **funding ecosystem**: philanthropists & **HNIs**, corporates
  (CSR), grant-making orgs, family institutions, retail donors.
- **Team ShikshaLokam** (internal).

> **Collaborative philanthropy** is the load-bearing funding message: SL brings *diverse*
> philanthropies, HNIs and institutions together to fund + build the movement **collectively, not in
> silos** — the comms face of the strategy note's "Fund Orchestration" capability
> ([[shikshalokam-2-strategy-note-jul2025]]).

## Channel plan (Tab 2) — brief

- **Owned:** Social (LI/X/YT), Newsletter (quarterly *Bit & Bytes* + themed + dialogue emailers),
  Website (trusted go-to, global-appeal UI/UX).
- **Earned:** print/online media (PR agency + organic), awards (target **1 global + 1 national/yr**),
  work-showcase opportunities.
- **Projects:** dialogues (**InvokED** once/yr, Shiksha Samvad as required), support to other teams
  (brand alignment).
- **Yearly success indicators:** Brand Survey; # advocacy partnerships (national + global); mentions
  on partners'/co-builders' channels; partner referrals/tags.
