## What IEFG is, and the gate

The International Education Funders Group: *"the largest global network of philanthropic actors
funding education… a learning and collaborating network, not a grantmaking institution. Membership
is for foundations and philanthropic organisations who give grants to other organisations; it is not
open to grantseekers or NGOs seeking funding."* Heard about it through IEFG's own leadership and
**Synergos**.

## The eligibility problem — Sonal has recorded it in the doc herself

> ShikshaLokam Foundation is a **Section 8 non-profit whose operational costs are covered by SFPI.**
> That makes SL a recipient of philanthropy and an implementer, not a grantmaker. The structurally
> eligible entity is **SFPI, not ShikshaLokam** — SFPI holds the endowment and funds SL, so on
> IEFG's definition SFPI is the funder and SL is the grantee.

Reinforcing this from outside: the Oxford GO Lab playbook
([[golab-oxford-shikshagraha-playbook-2026]]) publicly describes SL as *"an NGO founded under the
aegis of Shibulal Family Philanthropic Initiatives"* and an *"education leadership catalyst"* —
independent, published, and not the language of a grantmaker.

**"We grant-make" is currently ticked** and **Q2e — % of the education portfolio granted to external
organisations — is deliberately blank.** That number decides the application. The unanswered
sub-question: do Momentum Partners receive funds *from* SL, or bring their own and align?

Three routes were put to Sonal: apply as SFPI; ask IEFG first where an operating philanthropy fits;
or don't tick the grantmaking box. **Undecided.**
