# Bihar Department of Education — MoUs with seven organisations (2026)

The **Department of Education, Government of Bihar** signed MoUs **at zero cost** with **seven**
educational organisations to strengthen quality and modern education. Announced via an official
Bihar government video; transcript shared by Ayush → user-validated. Raw: [[../../raw/2026-07-17]].

## The seven signatories + their roles
| Organisation | Role under the MoU |
|---|---|
| **ekStep Foundation** | Digital practice in Hindi, English & Maths |
| **Wadhwani AI** | Assess young children's reading ability; identify students lagging in learning |
| **Khan Academy India** | Strengthen Maths & Science for classes 6–10 |
| **Pi Jam Foundation** | Digital skills, Artificial Intelligence & coding |
| **Mantra for Change** | Experiential education via project-based learning + mentoring across schools |
| **Pratham Education Foundation** | Improve foundational learning (language & maths) |
| **ShikshaLokam Foundation** | Connect the voices of **parents, teachers & students** to the system via an **AI-based framework** |

**Stated aim:** timely identification of learning gaps, regular practice, and continuous improvement
in education quality.

## ShikshaLokam's role (the listening angle)
SL's MoU role is **listening at scale**: an AI-based way to bring parent / teacher / student feedback
into the education system so those closest to each child are heard and become partners in learning.
This is the [[mitra-listening-at-scale-part1-2026]] thread, now formalised with a state department.
