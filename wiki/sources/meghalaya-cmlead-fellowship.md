# Meghalaya CMLEAD Fellowship

The Chief Minister Leadership in Education And Development Fellowship, Meghalaya. A state-owned
initiative of the Department of Education, Government of Meghalaya, proposed by Mantra4Change to the
State Project Director, Samagra Shiksha Meghalaya in March 2026. Extracted from the proposal document
and the launch messaging doc; no synthesis.

## What it is

Twelve Fellows, one per administrative district, embedded inside the district education system for
one year (extendable to two, on performance), supported by State Program Managers. Fellows are
administratively attached to districts, functionally accountable to the Commissioner Secretary /
SPD, Samagra Shiksha. All communication is framed as a state-owned initiative; Mantra4Change is the
knowledge and implementation support partner, not the programme owner.

The system it works in: roughly 8,000 government and aided schools across 12 districts, much of it
remote and difficult to reach. The gap the Fellowship answers is capacity, not intent. District and
state officials are absorbed by routine administration, which limits deep planning, tracking and
course correction on the reforms that touch classrooms.

## What Fellows do

- Bridge academic and administrative units: DSEOs and DIETs, MTTA / DERT / DSEL / Samagra Shiksha,
  state planning teams and district implementation units.
- Anchor a small number of priority themes: Foundational Literacy and Numeracy, Learning Enhancement
  Programs, teacher mentoring and classroom practice, school leadership development, vocational and
  skills orientation. District leadership can flex the focus.
- Support learning-focused monitoring: interpret student learning data, spot classroom-level
  implementation gaps, generate actionable insight. Explicitly not compliance reporting.
- Support school and community engagement (parent-teacher meetings, reading campaigns).
- Build system memory against staff turnover: good practices, challenges and solutions, district
  playbooks.

Lifecycle: recruitment and selection, structured induction and system immersion, field immersion on
a review-reflection cadence (monthly forums with the Commissioner / SPD, biannual interactions with
the Hon'ble Education Minister), continuous professional development and mentorship, quarterly
developmental performance reviews. The learning model is called Field-to-Forum.

## Who is eligible

Age 22 to 35 as on the last date of application, with relaxation for reserved categories per
government norms. Postgraduate degree in any discipline, or a graduate degree with at least two
years of relevant experience, preferably in education, development, public policy or governance.
Desired competencies: strong analytical and communication skills, comfort with fieldwork and travel,
ability to work with government systems, and core values such as empathy, integrity and adaptability.

## Cost (indicative, from the proposal)

Stipend Rs 40,000 per Fellow per month plus Rs 20,000 allowances, so Rs 7.2 lakh per Fellow per year.
Total annual estimate for 12 Fellows: Rs 92.4 lakh including programme management, excluding one-time
and variable costs. Year 1 shared roughly 50:50 between the Department and Mantra4Change, moving
progressively towards full state ownership.

## Launch messaging (the voices already used)

Three versions of the call for applications exist, written for the Hon'ble Chief Minister, the
Hon'ble Education Minister and the SPD. Their shared register: plain, warm, first-person, addressed
directly to young people across India, and closing on the same phrase — *lead, act, and transform*.
The SPD's version opens "Dear Youth" and leads with the children rather than the programme. This is
the register to match for any further candidate-facing communication in a department voice.

## First cohort (2026)

- 1,398 applications received from across India.
- Screening ran longer than planned; candidate communication was delayed as a result.
- Interviews conclude by the last week of July 2026.
- The 12 Fellows are announced in the first week of August 2026.
- Only shortlisted candidates are contacted; all further selection communication goes to them alone.

Original timeline in the proposal was April 2026 for approvals and the public call, May/June for
selection, July/August for induction and deployment, January 2027 for mid-term review.
