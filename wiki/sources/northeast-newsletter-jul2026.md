# Northeast newsletter draft, July 2026

Subject line: *Building momentum across Meghalaya and Nagaland*. Signed **Santosh More**. A partner
and supporter update on the Northeast portfolio. Read-only source — a Doc handed over, not edited.
Facts below are extracted, not synthesised.

## The four milestones it reports

**CMLEAD Fellowship, Meghalaya — response numbers.**
Over **1,400 applications**. **50 candidates shortlisted** for interview after screening.
**Interviews on 30 and 31 July 2026.** Framed as evidence that young professionals are willing to
work alongside government systems, not around them.

**MoUs signed with the Government of Meghalaya.**
Formal signing of **both** the CMLEAD Fellowship and the **Project-Based Learning (PBL)** MoUs,
between the Department of School Education, Government of Meghalaya, and **Mantra4Change**.

**PBL co-creation workshop, Meghalaya.**
Concluded, with **Mathematics and Science teachers plus Department of School Education officials**
in the room together. The framing that matters: the programme was *not* designed in isolation, so
the approach is rooted in Meghalaya's classroom realities and the state's stated priorities.

**Nagaland — partnership moves to implementation.**
**Proposal approved, MoU formally signed.** **State- and district-level orientation and
capacity-building programmes completed**, establishing shared understanding before ground rollout.

## The closing argument

The draft's own thesis: these are not individual achievements but *momentum* across the Northeast,
built on three things — **government partnerships, collaborative programme design, and investment
in educational leadership.**

## Notes for anyone drafting from this

- CMLEAD numbers here are **fresher than** [[meghalaya-cmlead-fellowship]], which carries the
  programme design and launch messaging. Use that file for what the Fellowship *is*; use this one
  for where the cohort **selection** has reached.
- Two things are consistently paired in this portfolio and should stay paired in any retelling:
  **CMLEAD (leadership) and PBL (classroom practice)** — leadership development plus what actually
  changes for children.
- The recurring Northeast proof point is **co-creation with government, not delivery to it.** It
  appears in the PBL workshop block and in the Nagaland orientation block, and it is the strongest
  claim the region's story makes.
