# Media coverage index — ShikshaLokam & Shikshagraha

Canonical full dataset (2,548 rows, sortable/filterable, newest-first):
**[Media Coverage Tracker (Google Sheet)](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1VgpW7HPRE6ckds6mMowNs9zCq9AkyW4CPq2HvzXc2n0/edit)** — columns: Date · Entity · Story/Event · Title · Outlet · Type · Coverage · URL.

Compiled 2026-07-18 from two internal Coverage Dossiers (Shikshagraha, ShikshaLokam) + a public
WebSearch sweep, deduped by normalized URL.

## The dataset at a glance
- **Rows:** 2,548 unique links · **date range:** Feb 2020 → Jul 2026.
- **Entity split:** Shikshagraha ~2,289 · ShikshaLokam ~244 · joint/founder ~15.
- **Type:** Online 2,314 · Social 157 · Print 38 · Clipping 35 · Video 4.
- **Reality check:** the two biggest "domains" are `lion.box.com` (140) + `drive.google.com` (73) —
  internal clipping stores, not live coverage. A large share of "outlets" are ANI/PTI syndication
  microsites (same release, many mastheads). **Independent editorial pickup is a small subset.**

## Event clusters covered (Story/Event column filters to these)
- **InvokED** editions — 3.0 (2024), 4.0 (Mar 2025), 5.0 (Feb 2026).
- **Shikshagraha Awards** — 2025 (InvokED 4.0), 2026 (announced Feb 2026; nominations Jun 2026, closed 31 Jul 2026).
- **Shiksha Samvaad** — Mumbai (Oct 2024), Patna/Bihar (Jan 2025), April 2025, Delhi IncluED, Nagaland (Mar 2026), Nagapattinam (Oct 2025), CWS (Nov 2025).
- **Cannes Lions 2026** — SDG/gender-equality shortlist (Jun 2026).
- **AWS partnership** (Oct 2024) · **DNDA / DB / PBL** state PR pushes (Oct–Nov 2025).

## Treat as volume, not proof
- ANI/PTI syndication microsite network (Dubai City Reporter, European Sun Times, Richmond Evening
  News, British News Network, US World Today, Federal Despatch, etc.) — same wire release.
- `lion.box.com` / `drive.google.com` rows — internal clippings & screenshots, not public links.

## Known gaps
- Per-row dates exist for ~2,136 rows; ~412 use the **event month inferred from the source tab**
  (flagged `date≈event month` in the Coverage column). 412 FY-range/undated rows carry no date.
- Metadata uneven: PR tabs often gave only a publication name → some Title cells show a domain.
- Not exhaustive of print/regional-language beyond what the dossiers logged.
