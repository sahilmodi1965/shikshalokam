# InvokED Impact & Perception Survey — the build sheet

The build sheet ("product requirement document") for a **MItra bot that runs the InvokED Impact &
Perception Survey** as a conversation, not a form, across everyone who came to InvokED. Shared into the brain by
Ayush on 2026-08-07 as the reference for a comms campaign, so treated as user-validated org
material.

**Source:** Google Sheet, 7 tabs — `Overview` · `Questions_Revised_10` · `Dashboard_Metrics_Revised`
· `Report_Metrics_Revised` · plus three superseded tabs (`Questions`, `Dashboard_Metrics`,
`Report_Metrics/Templates`). The `_Revised` tabs are the live ones.

## The strategic frame (verbatim intent)

InvokED is ShikshaLokam's **global dialogue on education leadership**. Across **5 editions** it has
reached **15+ geographies, 400+ organisations, 250+ leaders/experts, 1,000+ registrations, 800+
attendees**.

> "Having built visibility, InvokED is now moving **from visibility to credibility** — positioning
> itself as a **convening-to-action platform** that drives collaborations, catalyses partnerships and
> enables tangible outcomes for systemic change in education."

The survey exists to evidence that claim: a pulse-check across speakers + participants capturing
**what happened AFTER the events**. Global scope (India + international), everyone who attended one
or more of InvokED 1.0–5.0.

## Audience — the inverse of MItra's usual persona

InvokED alumni: senior education-sector professionals — practitioners, funders, researchers,
government officials, civil-society and philanthropy leaders, social entrepreneurs, academics,
edtech leaders. India and global. **Highly literate, digitally fluent, English-comfortable and
time-poor.** The sheet flags this openly as *"the INVERSE of MItra's usual grassroots persona — so
the bot must be crisp, professional and respectful of their time, never simplistic or hand-holding."**

- **Base:** ~800–1,000 registrants/attendees across 5 editions.
- **Target:** **150–250 completed conversations.**
- **Language:** English only.
- **Persona:** Interviewer (warm, professional) — a sharp researcher running a brief impact
  interview. Not "friend", not "mentor", not a rigid form.
- **Length claim:** ~5 minutes.

## The 8 questions

| Q | Section | The question (abbrev.) | Format | Probe |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Participant Profile | Role, org type, where you're based | Open | No |
| 2 | Editions Attended | Which of 1.0–5.0 | Multi-select tap (pre-fill from registrant record, confirm only) | No |
| 3 | Perception Shift | Has InvokED shifted how you think about collective action / education leadership / system change? | Tap: No change / Somewhat / Significantly | **Yes ×1, branched** |
| 4 | Action & Outcome | Has any of it led to something substantial — collaboration, pilot, funding, partnership, a decision taken differently? | Tap: Yes / In progress / No | **Yes ×1, branched** |
| 5 | Ripple & Influence | Beyond a formal project — has anything travelled? Idea to a team, an introduction made, something spoken or written, a way of working changed | Open (1–3 sentences) + tap: Not at all / Somewhat / Significantly | No |
| 6 | Timing & Continuity | When did it start — weeks / months / later? Still going? | Two taps | No |
| 7 | Network & Continuity | Would you take part again? What would make staying connected year-round worth your time? | Tap Yes/Maybe/No + open | No |
| 8 | Future Readiness | "Future readiness" — what does it actually mean in your work, and what are you doing that moves it forward? | Open, two parts, 2–4 sentences | No |

**Q4 is the critical question** — the sheet calls the follow-up question "the key value-add": it turns *"Yes, I
collaborated"* into a structured **Action Story** (who / what / outcome).

**Opening line:** *"Hello, and thank you for being part of InvokED. I'm Mitra. Over the next few
minutes I'd love to hear what InvokED has meant for you, and anything that's happened since. There
are no right or wrong answers — just share in your own words. Shall we begin?"*

**Closing line:** *"Thank you. This is exactly the kind of insight that helps us show what InvokED
makes possible. Your voice will feed directly into the InvokED Impact Report — and we hope to see
you at the next edition."*

Optional, skippable **photo upload** at the end (a collaboration, pilot or InvokED moment).

## What it produces

**Per-conversation report** (auto-generated): profile · editions · perception shift · action &
collaboration · partner origin · latent action · ripple · influence on work · timing & durability ·
network & continuity · future readiness · **one pull-quote**.

**Aggregate impact report** blocks: *What InvokED set in motion* · *It did not stop at two days* ·
credibility metrics · partner origin · the many ways it helped · narrative assets · future-readiness
narrative · forum & partnership leads · segments · ambassadors · recommendations.

**Dashboard metrics (28)** include: total responses · persona mix · geographic reach · repeat
participation · perception shift % · **any post-event effect** (Q4+Q5 — "the single number behind
'InvokED is not a two-day event'") · substantial change initiated / in progress · types of change ·
**funding unlocked** · **met at InvokED vs already known** · cross-sector partnerships · pilots
initiated · ripple types · ripple among non-collaborators · reach beyond attendees · action timeline
· still live · org-level influence · latent collaborations & barriers · network continuity · return
intent · year-round engagement demand · future-readiness definitions & contributions · ambassadors.

## Five honesty rules the sheet says must be printed with the report

These bind anything we write off this data:

1. **A 'No' on action is not an absence of impact.** Q4 asks only about *substantial* action. Q5
   captures effects that never became a project. Always read the two together; **never report the Q4
   No on its own.**
2. **Perception shift is self-reported.** The survey does not measure a before/after change. State
   that rather than implying a measured delta.
3. **Network continuity is a count**, not a percentage — no single question is asked of everyone.
4. **Partner origin is partially captured** — it sits inside a multi-part open answer. Report as a
   split of the responses that carry it, and state the base.
5. **Timing is respondent-recalled** — indicative of pattern, not a verified date.

## Why this matters to comms

- The survey is the **evidence engine for the credibility claim** — and therefore for InvokED 6.0's
  positioning (`projects/invoked-6/page.md`; 6.0 is 26–27 Feb 2027, PCPA Bengaluru).
- It also feeds **Shikshagraha Awards outreach** and narrative assets.
- **Q8 (future readiness) is the only question that gives the respondent something back** — an
  aggregate read on how 150–250 senior leaders define a term everyone uses differently. That is the
  campaign's strongest incentive to participate.
- The sheet is thorough on what the survey *takes* and says nothing about what the person answering
  *gets*. Closing that gap is our job, not the product team's.
