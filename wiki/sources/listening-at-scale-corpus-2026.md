# Listening at Scale / MItra — everything we have

A single map of the Listening at Scale thread — the thesis, the tool, the numbers, the assets, and
**who worked on what** — gathered for the LS campaign (task #2). Sweep run 2026-07-30 across the brain
repo + team Drive (129 Drive hits triaged to the core below).

## The thesis + tool (one line each)
- **Listening at Scale** = surfacing grassroots wisdom *at scale* so system decisions aren't made "on
  incomplete information." India's 1.5 lakh schools hold invisible solutions. Image: *"treasure
  scattered across the country, but no map to find it."*
- **MItra** = voice-enabled, multilingual AI listening tool — *"talks like a friend, not a
  never-ending questionnaire."* Anchor line: **"Not a survey. Not a form. A companion."**

## Canonical impact numbers (from the designed carousel `mitra-impact.pdf`)
- **32,742 chaupals** — "Conversations held, not forms filled."
- **8.79 lakh members** — "Voices in the room."
- **79,148 challenges surfaced** — "Named by the people living them."
- (Supersedes older collateral's "28,000+ chaupals / 8 lakh+".) Field Samvaads added **200+ voices**
  across 5 events (Nagaland, Delhi, Arunachal, Odisha, Villupuram).
